# Slide

A 16:9 slide in the Night theme: logo top left, a kicker naming the section, a `display` title, at most three short lines, one visual, the barrier stripe and the slide number at the foot.

- Built at 1280 × 720 (scale to 1920 × 1080 by doubling paddings to `space-16`).
- One idea per slide; copy at 24 px or larger; numbers only from real measurements.
- Screenshots of the console sit in a `surface` frame with `radius-lg` and a 1 px `line` border.
